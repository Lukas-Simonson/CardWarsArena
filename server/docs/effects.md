# Card effect model

Printed card data (`data/cards/*.json`) is generated by the importer and must
not be hand-edited. Rules behaviour lives in **`data/effects/*.json`**, a map of
card id to a list of effects, merged into the catalog at load time. A card is
*modelled* when it has an entry there (an empty list is valid: vanilla or
flavor-text cards). Decks may only contain modelled cards unless a game is
created with `AllowUnimplemented`.

## Effect kinds

| kind        | meaning |
|-------------|---------|
| `static`    | Always on while the card is in play: stat `mods` and/or a `restrict`ion |
| `trigger`   | Fires `on` an event: `enter_play`, `leave_play`, `turn_start`, `change_lanes`, `creature_enters`, `deals_hero_damage`, `deals_damage` (a Fight dealt it damage to a Creature or player), `attacks` (just activated to Fight, before damage) and `discarded` (went from play to a discard pile, not to a hand) and `fight_start` (its controller's Fight Phase begins) |
| `floop`     | `FLOOP >>>` ability: exhaust (Ready only), then run `do`. `cost` adds Actions to pay |
| `activate`  | `Pay N Action(s) >>>` ability, usable any number of times. `cost` 0 is allowed when the first op removes the card itself (`destroy_creature`/`bounce` with `self`) or `discard`s a `hand_card` target ("Discard a card >>>"). `limit` caps the uses each turn |
| `play_condition` | The card may only be played while `if` holds (Hot Eyebat, Bone Wand) |
| `cost_mod`  | The card costs `reduce` less: Actions and Landscapes, or only Actions with `actions_only` |
| `spell`     | Resolved when the Spell is played, then the Spell is discarded |
| `additional_cost` | Paid when the card is played (Field Reaper discards a card, Deforestation exhausts a Creature); its targets travel in the action's `cost_targets` |

## Pieces

- **Value** `{n, per?}`: a number, or `n` times a **Count**.
- **Count** `{of, scope?, type?, adjacent?, other?, div?, card?, from_target?}` where `of` is one of
  `landscapes`, `landscape_types`, `creatures`, `flooped_creatures`,
  `spells_played`, `opponent_hand`, `discard` (your discard pile), `empty_lanes`,
  `entered_this_turn` (Creatures that entered play this turn; `scope: any` for both sides),
  `entered_adjacent` (entered on your side in the Lanes next to the card), `base_attack`
  (the card's printed ATK), `milled_cost` (the cost of the card `mill` last discarded),
  `milled_spells` (how many Spells it discarded), `damage` (on the card, or on each chosen
  Creature with `from_target`), `damage_dealt` (what a Creature just did in a Fight), `buildings`
  (the Buildings you control, or only those of the card named by `card`), `played_titled`
  (cards you played this turn whose name has one of `words`) and `drawn_this_turn`.
  `div` counts whole groups ("for every 5 cards": `div: 5`), `card` limits `creatures` to one
  card, and `from_target` counts from each chosen Creature instead of the card. Landscape counts only see face-up ones.
  `creatures` with `type` matches the Creature's Landscape types *now* (see "Landscape types"
  below): a Rainbow Creature is never a Cornfield Creature.
- **Selector** `{rel, landscape?, changed_lanes?, damage?, flooped?, with_building?}`: which creatures. `rel` is
  `self`, `lane_ally` (my creature in this Lane), `lane_foe` (the opposing creature in this
  Lane), `adjacent_allies`, `your`, `opposing`, `any`. `flooped` picks Flooped (or only not Flooped) Creatures, `with_building` those in a Lane where
  their controller has a Building. `damage` is a `{min?, max?}` range the
  creature's damage must be in ("exactly 2 damage", "no damage").
- **Cond** `{opposing_creature_in_lane?, landscapes?{type,min?,max?}, building_in_lane?,
  opponent_hand_max?, discard_min?, controls?, on_landscape?, entered_max?, damage?, replaced?}`
  (`damage` is on the card itself, `replaced` is whether it replaced a Creature when it entered).
  Also `flooped` (the card is Flooped), `other_creatures` (a `{min?, max?}` range for how many Creatures
  you control besides the card) and `played` (`{landscape, min}`: cards of that type you played this turn).
- **Watch** (on a `creature_enters` trigger) `{landscape?, other?, your_turn?}`: which Creatures entering
  under your control fire it. `once_per_turn` limits the trigger to once each turn.
- **TargetSpec**: one choice slot. `what` = `creature|building|landscape|lane|discard_card|hand_card`,
  `side` = `you|opponent|any`, `in_lane`, `adjacent_lane`, `other` (not the card's own Lane),
  `lane_of` (only the Lane of an earlier slot's choice), `group` (slots sharing one need exactly
  one choice between them: "heal or deal 1 damage"), `distinct` (slots of one name may not pick
  the same target), `label` (what the choice is for; sent with the legal action as `labels`), `landscape`, `face_down`,
  `without` (for lanes: `creature` or `building`), `ready` and `damaged` (creatures), `card_type`, `optional`, and
  `count` (a Value: choose up to that many *distinct* targets).
- **Op**: one step of `do`: `draw`, `damage_creature`, `damage_hero`,
  `mod_creature`, `move_creature`, `move_building`, `flip_landscape`,
  `ready_creature`, `bounce`, `destroy_building`, `gain_actions`,
  `return_from_discard`, `put_on_deck`, `discard`, `copy_floop` (Schoolhouse),
  `steal_play` (Silo of Truth), `destroy_creature`, `mill`, `discard_choice`,
  `discount_next_creature`, `borrow_creature` (Spirit Tower), `ogre_gas`, `may`, `heal_creature`
  (`amount` is up to that much; 99 heals it all), `heal_hero` (never above 25), `swap_creatures`
  (the card itself and slot), `protect` (Cave of Solitude), `no_combat_damage` (Falling Star),
  `discount_replace` (Pyramidia), `upkeep` (Steakchop), `exhaust` (Ready Creatures become
  Flooped), `play_building` (a Building from a discard pile into a Lane), `search_building`
  (Deforestation), `move_damage` (Friendship Bracelet), `make_rainbow` (until end of turn),
  `rainbow_hand` (a card in hand is Rainbow until end of turn), `rebound` (Impossible Possum) and
  `redirect_attack` (Pied Piper), `freeze` (put a frozen token on the Landscapes named by `slot`, or the card's own
  with `self`; `both` also freezes the one opposite), `thaw` (take the token off the card's own Landscape) and
  `move_self` (see "Frozen tokens"). `discard_choice` may carry `rainbow_actions`: Actions gained if a
  Rainbow card is among those discarded. Ops reference
  target slots by index and may carry an `if` condition. `self` and `entering` act on the
  card itself or on the Creature that fired a `creature_enters` trigger instead of a slot;
  `permanent` makes `mod_creature` last while the Creature is in play.

## Frozen tokens

A Landscape may hold one frozen token (`Lane.Frozen`). It keeps its type and the token stays when it is flipped.

- No Creature may be played onto a frozen Landscape, and a Creature on one cannot be replaced (`ErrFrozen`). A card
  with the `play_on_frozen` restriction (Frosted Deanimator) may go onto an *empty* frozen Landscape, but not over a
  Creature. Buildings and Spells are not affected, and Creatures can be moved onto or off a frozen Landscape.
- Freezing a frozen Landscape does nothing. Both `freeze` and `thaw` work on one Landscape at a time.
- A player removes a token on their own side with the `thaw` action: discard one card from their hand, in the main
  phase, no Actions. Snow Baller's own ability removes the token on its Landscape instead (`thaw` with `self` as the
  first op, so it costs no Actions), and only while it is frozen (`lane_frozen: "own"`).
- Counts: `frozen_landscapes` (with `scope: "any"` for both sides), `exhausted_creatures` (Flooped or Fought) and
  `destroyed` (what the last `destroy_creature` destroyed). **Selector** `frozen` picks Creatures on a frozen (or,
  set to false, unfrozen) Landscape; **Cond** `lane_frozen` is `own` (its Landscape) or `both` (both in its Lane),
  `frozen_landscapes` a range for all the tokens in play, `your_turn`, `hp` (its controller's) and
  `lane_ally_exhausted` (their Creature in its Lane is exhausted).
- **Target** `exhausted` picks exhausted Creatures. **Restrict** `no_ready`: does not Ready at the start of its
  controller's turn (Fatigued Librarian, Weakened Warrior; they enter play Flooped with `exhaust` on `self`, and
  `ready_creature` on `self` when they change Lanes).
- `move_self` (`to: "empty_own"`, optionally `else_to: "empty_opponent"`, and `else` ops for when there is nowhere)
  moves the Creature to an empty Landscape at the start of the turn: with one place to go it goes, with several its
  controller chooses (a `move` Choice), and with none the `else` ops run (Drained Cleric discards, Woadic Ring Leader is
  destroyed, Tired Wombat tries the opponent's side).

## Landscape types, Rainbow and Buildings

A Creature's Landscape types are worked out each time they are asked for (`creatureTypes`): its card's
own type, but only Rainbow when a Rainbow rule applies (Charming City's Lane, or `Creature.Rainbow`
from Puppy Parade), plus Rainbow for a card with the `also_rainbow` restriction (Tiny Elephant).
A card in hand is Rainbow if it is printed that way or Woadic Weirdo has made it so
(`State.RainbowHand`); that matters for what it costs to play, for Infant Scholar and for Fiddling Ferret.

`static` effects with a `restrict` do rule-changing jobs: `lane_rainbow`, `also_rainbow`,
`lane_no_floop`, `extra_buildings`, `silence_opposing_buildings`, `counts_as_landscape` (with `as`) and
`copy_adjacent_buildings`. A Mod with the stat `damage_taken` raises every amount of damage the Creatures it
selects take (Blood Castle).

A Lane normally holds one Building. While a Creature with `extra_buildings` is in the Lane, Buildings played
there are added as `Lane.Extra` instead of replacing the one there. Everything that looks at Buildings
(statics, counts, triggers, Landscape counts) sees all of them; abilities, targets, destroying and moving
only deal with the first, and when it leaves the next one takes its place. Playing a Building in a Lane
without such a Creature replaces all of them.

## Targets are chosen up front

A player supplies the targets inside the action that uses the card
(`play_card`, `floop`, `activate`), one list per slot, validated against the
spec. Rules from the rulebook:

- A mandatory slot with no legal choice means a Spell or ability cannot be used.
- A trigger whose mandatory target has no legal choice simply does nothing.
- `optional` slots may be left empty ("you may ...").

## Owner versus controller

Every card instance has an `owner` (whose deck it came from). Control follows
the side of the board a card sits on. Cards that die, are replaced, destroyed
or returned go to their **owner's** discard pile or hand, so a Creature taken
with Field Reaper dies back to its original player. A Creature moved onto the
opposing side changes controller and stays exhausted until its new
controller's next turn.

## Abilities are per-creature state

All reads of a Creature's rules go through `effectsOf`. A Creature can be
`silenced` (no abilities) and, with `lent`, given only the Floop ability of
another card until end of turn (Schoolhouse). Statics, triggers and Floops all
respect that, so a silenced Husker Knight is a 0/0 and dies.

## Choices

Most targets are chosen up front, with the action. A few cards need a decision
from a player *during* resolution, and possibly from the other player. They open a
**Choice** (`State.Choice`; further ones wait in `State.Queue`), which stops the game
until it is answered, with `choose` actions from the player who decides:

- `discard_choice` (`who`, `amount`): that player picks the cards to discard from their hand.
  If they would discard their whole hand there is nothing to choose, and it just happens.
- `ogre_gas`: the top 3 cards of your deck come out of it and are shown to both players; you
  put one on the bottom and the rest are discarded. With one card left it goes to the bottom.

Optional effects do not stop the game. `may` (`targets`, `then`) puts an **offer** on the
Creature (`Creature.offered`): a free `activate` ability, listed after the card's own, that runs
`then` on the chosen `targets`. It lasts until its controller does anything else (draw, play a
card, end the main phase, use another ability), so a "you may" at the start of your turn shows up
on the card, like a Floop, instead of interrupting every turn. Using one leaves the others.

## Pending free plays

Silo of Truth takes a random card from the opponent's hand. Because the card is
unknown until then, its Lane and targets are chosen afterwards: the game enters
a pending state in which only `play_pending` is legal. The card is played for
free (no Actions, Landscapes or additional costs). A card that cannot legally
be played anywhere goes to its owner's discard pile so the game cannot get
stuck.

## Rulings chosen where the rulebook is silent

- Schoolhouse with no Creature that has a Floop in the discard pile does
  nothing (its own Floop is still spent).
- Field Reaper targets any Creature in its Lane (so it may move itself); with
  no empty adjacent Lane on your side the trigger does nothing.
- A moved opposing Creature is exhausted (not Flooped) until its new
  controller's next turn.
- Woad Mobile Home moves one of *your* Creatures from an adjacent Lane into its
  own Lane if that Lane has no Creature.
- Silo of Truth "no cost" waives the additional cost too.

- Ms. Mummy's "gains 1 DEF" is permanent, like Sand Eyebat's.
- "Target player" (Skeletal Hand, Strength Crystal) is the opponent, or you, as the card says:
  nobody benefits from pointing them the other way.
- "Heal up to" and "heal all damage" use `heal_creature`; damage is never removed by anything else.
- Cave of Solitude: its Creature cannot be targeted by the opponent's cards or attacked until your
  next turn starts, but you can still target it yourself.
- Pyramidia's discount applies to the next Creature played over your Creature in its Lane this turn.
- Steakchop: with fewer than 2 cards in hand it is simply destroyed. After discarding one card you
  must discard the second.
- Ms. Mummy's offer is available from the start of your turn until you do anything else.
- Palace of Bone stops the entering and leaving play triggers of Creatures opposite it,
  including other Creatures' "when a Creature enters play" triggers that they would set off.
- Spirit Tower needs an empty Lane on your side as well as the Creature to take; the Creature
  goes home to the same Lane at the end of the turn (if its owner has put nothing there).
- Zazo's Magic Seeds counts Creatures that entered play this turn on the target's side.

- Impossible Possum only returns when it goes from play to its owner's discard pile (dying, being
  replaced or destroyed), not when it is discarded from hand or deck. Its owner decides.
- "Exhaust" (Deforestation) makes a Ready Creature Flooped.
- Emboldened Retriever draws whenever it attacks: there is no reason to refuse.
- Pied Piper asks before the Fight, once a Flooped Creature of yours could take its place. The
  Flooped Creature stays Flooped, and fights the Creature opposite Pied Piper. Pied Piper is spent either way.
- Friendship Bracelet moves any amount of damage from one of your Creatures to another; "swap" in
  either direction is the same as moving the difference. Cards moved this way are not "dealt".
- Business Dog counts itself (it has "Dog" in its title), and Infant Scholar counts a card made Rainbow
  by Woadic Weirdo. Cards played for free (Silo of Truth) are not counted.
- Vampire Lord heals in combat only: damage from its own ability would need its own ruling.
- Learning Center has the rules of the Buildings beside it, once for each, so two Oil Refineries beside it
  give twice the bonus.
- A Hero's rules are played by the engine; see "Heroes" below.

All nine preconstructed decks (Finn's, Jake's, BMO's, Lady's, PB's, LSP's, Cake's, Fionna's and Gunter's) are fully modelled.

- Spazzy Cola gives +1 ATK to the Creatures in play when it is played, not to ones that enter later this turn.
- A "frozen Landscape" count (Icy Commando, Floating Ice Palace) counts the tokens on both sides of the board.
- Fatigued Librarian and Weakened Warrior are Flooped while they are exhausted, so they cannot be replaced until
  they Ready.

## Heroes

Each deck comes with a Hero, dealt into `Player.Hero` and in play for the whole game. A Hero is written
like any other card (`data/effects/heroes.json`) and may use `static`, `trigger` and `activate`
effects. It is not in a Lane, so it cannot use `floop`, and anything that reads "this card's Lane" does
not apply to it. Its source position is Lane -1.

- **Activated abilities** are offered as `activate` actions with `source: "hero"`. A Hero's `activate`
  may cost 0 Actions, and `limit` caps the uses each turn (Princess Bubblegum).
- **Triggers** on a Hero: `building_played` (its controller put a Building into play, by any means),
  `replaces` (its controller replaced a Creature on their turn) and `returns` (one of their Creatures went
  back to their hand from play or from their discard pile, on their turn). Unlike a Creature's trigger,
  a Hero's trigger honours `if` (it fires only while the condition holds) and `once_per_turn`.
- **Cond** `returned` is a `{min?, max?}` range for how many Creatures you returned this turn, and
  `landscape_types` one for how many face-up Landscape types you control. **Selector** `face_down` picks
  Creatures on a face-down (or, set to false, face-up) Landscape.
- **`grant`** (a static effect, Heroes only) `{to, ability}` gives a `floop` or `activate` ability to the
  Creatures its Selector picks, while it picks them (Finn). A Creature that already has a Floop ability of
  its own keeps using that one, and a Silenced Creature loses the granted ability too.
